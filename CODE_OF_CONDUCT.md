# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

TermGlide welcomes people with different backgrounds and levels of experience. Project
participation should be respectful, specific, and constructive. Good-faith disagreement is
welcome; harassment and personal attacks are not.

Examples of positive participation include:

- giving actionable technical feedback;
- respecting different viewpoints and experience levels;
- accepting responsibility and correcting mistakes; and
- keeping discussions focused on improving the project and its community.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention,
doxxing, deliberate disruption, and publishing another person's private information without
permission.

## Scope

This policy applies in repository issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews, and other spaces
where someone is representing TermGlide.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately through the contact options on the
[maintainer's GitHub profile](https://github.com/DAWNCR0W). Do not publish sensitive details in an
issue. The maintainer may edit or remove content and may warn, temporarily restrict, or permanently
ban participants whose behavior violates this policy.

Reports will be reviewed as promptly and impartially as practical. Information will be shared only
as needed to investigate and respond, subject to legal and safety obligations.
